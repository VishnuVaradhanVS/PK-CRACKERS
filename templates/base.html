IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTICE

Supreme Court directions on firecrackers

The Supreme Court's order dated 29 October 2026 permitted only a limited, temporary relaxation for approved green firecrackers in Delhi-NCR on 7 and 8 November 2026, subject to strict conditions. The complete ban in Delhi-NCR resumed on 9 November 2026.

Firecracker rules differ by State, Union Territory and local jurisdiction. This notice is not a statement that one identical ban applies throughout India. Before buying, storing, transporting or using any firecracker, check the latest directions issued by the Supreme Court and the relevant local authorities. Use only products permitted by law and follow all safety requirements.

Reference: Supreme Court of India, order dated 29 October 2026 in Arjun Gopal & Ors. v. Union of India & Ors.
